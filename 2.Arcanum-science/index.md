Arcanum Science is a historical and cultural encyclopedia of esoteric traditions, organised by the Esoteric Arcanum master taxonomy: A, the sacred sciences and their pictures of the cosmos; B, magic, divination and the grimoires; C, the schools and traditions that kept them. The pages describe what these traditions taught and how scholars study them; they are not instructions for practice.
