Sufi mysticism is the inner teaching of Sufism: the path through stations (*maqamat*) and states (*ahwal*) towards *fana*, the passing away of the ego in God. It is expressed in the writings of al-Ghazali and Ibn Arabi and the poetry of Rumi, Attar and Hafez, which are classics of world literature.
