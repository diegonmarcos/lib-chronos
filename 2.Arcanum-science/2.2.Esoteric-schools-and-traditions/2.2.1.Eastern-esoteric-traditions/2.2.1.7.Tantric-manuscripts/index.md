Tantric manuscripts are the large body of texts, in Sanskrit and other languages, that record tantric doctrine and ritual: the subtle body with its channels and chakras, mantras, and diagrams (*yantras* and *mandalas*). Many survive in Nepalese and Tibetan collections and are being catalogued by scholars.
