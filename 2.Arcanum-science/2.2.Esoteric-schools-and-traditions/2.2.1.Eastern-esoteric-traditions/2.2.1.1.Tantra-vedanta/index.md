Vedanta is the family of Hindu philosophies based on the Upanishads; Advaita Vedanta, set out by Shankara (eighth century), teaches that the self and the ultimate reality, *brahman*, are one. Tantra names ritual traditions, Hindu, Buddhist and Jain, that arose from about the fifth century CE, using mantra, diagram and initiation.
