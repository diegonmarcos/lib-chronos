Esoteric Buddhism, or Vajrayana, is the tantric form of Buddhism that arose in India around the seventh century and spread to Tibet, Mongolia, China and Japan, where it became the Shingon and Tendai schools. It uses mantra, mandala, visualisation and initiation by a teacher. It is the main form of Buddhism in Tibet.
