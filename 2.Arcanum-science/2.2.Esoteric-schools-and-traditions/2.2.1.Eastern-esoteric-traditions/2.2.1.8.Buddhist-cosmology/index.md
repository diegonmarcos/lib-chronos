Buddhist cosmology describes a universe of countless world systems, each with Mount Meru at its centre and realms ranging from hells to the heavens of the gods, arranged by states of mind. Beings move through them in the cycle of rebirth. It is set out in texts such as the *Abhidharmakosha*. Compare [[astronomy.cosmology]].
