Taoism (Daoism) is the Chinese tradition rooted in the *Tao Te Ching* attributed to Laozi and in the *Zhuangzi*, teaching harmony with the Tao, the way of nature. From the second century CE it also became an organised religion with priests, temples, scriptures and rites. See also [[beliefs.world-religions]].
