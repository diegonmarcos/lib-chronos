Sufism is the mystical tradition of Islam, organised from about the twelfth century into orders (*tariqas*) such as the Qadiriyya, Naqshbandiyya and the Mevlevi, whose turning ceremony is famous. Sufis practise remembrance of God (*dhikr*), follow a master and wrote much of the great poetry of Arabic, Persian and Turkish. See also [[beliefs.world-religions]].
