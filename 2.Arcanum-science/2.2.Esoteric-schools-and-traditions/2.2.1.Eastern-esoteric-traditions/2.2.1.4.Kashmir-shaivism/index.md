Kashmir Shaivism is a group of non-dual Hindu traditions that flourished in Kashmir from the ninth to the twelfth century, with Abhinavagupta as its greatest thinker. It teaches that the world is the free self-expression of Shiva as pure consciousness, with *spanda*, the divine vibration, as a key idea. It also produced an important theory of aesthetics.
