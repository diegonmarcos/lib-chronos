Taoist alchemy has two branches: external alchemy (*waidan*), the making of elixirs from minerals, which poisoned several Chinese emperors, and internal alchemy (*neidan*), which from the Tang and Song dynasties turned the same language into meditation and breathing practice. Joseph Needham's work traced its links to Chinese science.
