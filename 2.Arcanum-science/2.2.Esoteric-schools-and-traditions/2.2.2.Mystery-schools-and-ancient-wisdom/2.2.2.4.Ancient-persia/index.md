Ancient Persia is the home of Zoroastrianism, the religion of Zarathustra, with its sacred texts the *Avesta*, its opposition of truth and the lie, and its priests, the Magi. It was the state religion of the Achaemenid and Sasanian empires and shaped ideas of judgement and the end of time. See also [[beliefs.world-religions]].
