Mesopotamian religion honoured gods such as Anu, Enlil, Enki, Inanna/Ishtar and Marduk in city temples and ziggurats. Its myths include the *Epic of Gilgamesh* and the creation poem *Enuma Elish*, among the oldest literature in the world. See also [[beliefs.mythology]].
