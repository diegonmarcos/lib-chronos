Mesoamerican civilisations, Olmec, Maya and Aztec among them, built complex calendar systems (the 260-day ritual count and the 365-day year), tracked Venus and eclipses, and aligned cities to the sky. Their cosmology placed the present world in a series of created and destroyed ages. See also [[astronomy.observation]].
