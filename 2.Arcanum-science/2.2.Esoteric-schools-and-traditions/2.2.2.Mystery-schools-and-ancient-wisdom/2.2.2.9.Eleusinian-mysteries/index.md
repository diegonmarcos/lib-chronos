The Eleusinian Mysteries were held each year at Eleusis near Athens for nearly two thousand years, in honour of Demeter and her daughter Persephone. Initiates fasted, walked in procession from Athens and saw secret rites in the hall of the Telesterion, which promised a happier afterlife. Emperor Theodosius closed the sanctuary at the end of the fourth century.
