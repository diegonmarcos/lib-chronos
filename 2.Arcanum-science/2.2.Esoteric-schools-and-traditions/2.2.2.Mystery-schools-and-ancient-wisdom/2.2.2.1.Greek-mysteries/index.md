The Greek mysteries were cults that admitted members by secret initiation and promised a better fate after death: those of Eleusis, Samothrace, Dionysus and the Orphics. Initiates were forbidden to reveal the rites, so modern knowledge comes from hints in literature, inscriptions and art. See also [[beliefs.mythology]].
