Egyptian religion had temple priesthoods, secret rites and an elaborate literature of the afterlife: the Pyramid Texts, the Coffin Texts and the *Book of the Dead*. Greek and Roman writers saw Egypt as the home of hidden wisdom, an image later Hermeticism and Freemasonry drew on. Egyptology studies the actual sources. See also [[anthropology.archaeology]].
