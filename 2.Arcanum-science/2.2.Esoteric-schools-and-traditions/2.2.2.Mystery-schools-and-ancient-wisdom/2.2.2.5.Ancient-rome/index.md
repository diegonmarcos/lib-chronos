Roman religion was a state matter: priestly colleges read omens by augury, the Sibylline Books were consulted in crises, and the empire absorbed cults from Egypt, Persia and Asia Minor, such as those of Isis, Mithras and Cybele. This mixing is called syncretism and is a major theme of the history of religions.
