Initiation rites mark the passage from one state to another, often by symbolic death and rebirth. Arnold van Gennep's *Rites of Passage* (1909) described their three stages of separation, transition and incorporation, and anthropologists have found them in many societies, as well as in the ancient mystery cults. See also [[anthropology.cultural]].
