Ancient India produced the Vedas (from about 1500 BCE), hymns for sacrificial ritual, and the Upanishads, philosophical texts on the self and *brahman*. Ascetic and yogic movements of the first millennium BCE gave rise to Buddhism and Jainism. See also [[beliefs.philosophy]].
