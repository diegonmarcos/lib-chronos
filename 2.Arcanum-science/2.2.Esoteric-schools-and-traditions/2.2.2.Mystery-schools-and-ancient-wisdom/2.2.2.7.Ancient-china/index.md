Ancient China's earliest writing appears on Shang dynasty oracle bones (about 1200 BCE), used to put questions to ancestors. Shamans (*wu*), the *I Ching*, and the cosmological schools of yin and yang and the five phases shaped Chinese thought before and alongside Confucianism and Taoism.
