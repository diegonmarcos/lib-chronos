Mesopotamian scholars of Sumer, Babylon and Assyria kept vast cuneiform libraries of omens, astronomy, medicine and incantation, such as that of Ashurbanipal at Nineveh. Their systematic records of the sky laid foundations for later astronomy and astrology. See also [[astronomy.observation]].
