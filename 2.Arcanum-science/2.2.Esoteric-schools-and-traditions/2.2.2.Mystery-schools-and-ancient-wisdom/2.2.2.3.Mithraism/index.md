Mithraism was a Roman mystery cult of the god Mithras, popular among soldiers from the first to the fourth century CE. Members met in cave-like temples (*mithraea*) whose central image shows Mithras slaying a bull, and they rose through seven grades of initiation linked to the planets. Little of its doctrine was written down.
