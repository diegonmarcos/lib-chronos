The Orphic Hymns are 87 short Greek hymns, probably from the second or third century CE, attributed to the mythical poet Orpheus. Orphic texts more broadly taught that the soul is divine and bound in a cycle of rebirth; gold tablets buried with initiates give instructions for the afterlife.
