*Historical lineages, mystical philosophies, and ancient initiatory cults.*

Division C of the taxonomy groups the historical, cultural and philosophical lineages that developed and preserved these ideas over millennia: the Western esoteric currents, the mystical and tantric traditions of Asia and the Islamic world, and the mystery cults and wisdom literature of the ancient civilisations.
