Rosicrucianism began with three anonymous German manifestos (1614-1616) announcing a secret brotherhood of the Rosy Cross devoted to reform through Christian Hermetic and alchemical knowledge. No such order is known to have existed then, but the texts started a long debate and inspired later societies from the eighteenth century onward.
