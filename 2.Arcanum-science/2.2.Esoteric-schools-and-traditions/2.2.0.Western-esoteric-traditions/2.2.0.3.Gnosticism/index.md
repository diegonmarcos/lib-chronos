Gnosticism is the modern name for religious movements of the second and third centuries that taught salvation through *gnosis*, a revealed knowledge, often with a myth of a lesser creator god. The Nag Hammadi library, found in Egypt in 1945, gave scholars their own texts for the first time. See also [[beliefs.world-religions]].
