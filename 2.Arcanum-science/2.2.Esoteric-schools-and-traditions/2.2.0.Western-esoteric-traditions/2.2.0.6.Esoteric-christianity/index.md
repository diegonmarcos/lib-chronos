Esoteric Christianity is a name for readings of Christianity that claim a hidden, inner teaching beside the public one. Examples include Christian Kabbalah, the theosophy of Jacob Boehme, Swedenborg's visions and Rudolf Steiner's Anthroposophy. Church history generally treats these as movements on the edge of mainstream doctrine.
