Renaissance magic is the learned 'natural magic' of fifteenth- and sixteenth-century Europe: Marsilio Ficino's astral medicine, Pico della Mirandola's union of Kabbalah and philosophy, Cornelius Agrippa's *Three Books of Occult Philosophy* (1533) and Giordano Bruno. Frances Yates' studies made it a major theme of intellectual history.
