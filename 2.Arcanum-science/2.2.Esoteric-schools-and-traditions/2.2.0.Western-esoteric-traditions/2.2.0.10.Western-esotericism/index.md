Western esotericism is the academic field that studies, as history, the currents on this page and its neighbours: Hermeticism, Kabbalah, alchemy, Rosicrucianism, occultism and New Age. Antoine Faivre and Wouter Hanegraaff helped found it as a university discipline from the 1990s. See also [[beliefs.religion-studies]].
