tags: arcanum, symbolism, art, allegory

Mystical symbolism is the use of images to express religious or esoteric ideas that are hard to say in words: alchemical allegories, emblem books, the tarot's trumps, the Tree of Life, mandalas. It influenced Symbolist painting and poetry of the late nineteenth century. See also [[art.movements]].
