The Neoplatonic commentaries are the long expositions of Plato and Aristotle written by late antique philosophers such as Proclus, Simplicius and Olympiodorus, and later by Renaissance scholars such as Ficino. They preserved much of ancient philosophy and joined it to theology, both pagan and Christian.
