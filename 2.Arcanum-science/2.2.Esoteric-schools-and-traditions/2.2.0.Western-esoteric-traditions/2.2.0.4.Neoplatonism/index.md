Neoplatonism is the last great school of Greek philosophy, founded by Plotinus (third century CE): all things emanate from the One, through Intellect and Soul, and the soul can return to it by contemplation. Through Porphyry, Proclus and later Christian, Jewish and Islamic thinkers it shaped medieval and Renaissance metaphysics. See also [[beliefs.philosophy]].
