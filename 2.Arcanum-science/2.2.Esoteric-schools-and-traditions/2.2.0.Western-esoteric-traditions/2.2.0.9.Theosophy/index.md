Theosophy is the movement of the Theosophical Society, founded in New York in 1875 by Helena Blavatsky, Henry Olcott and William Judge. It taught a hidden wisdom behind all religions and helped bring Hindu and Buddhist ideas to Western audiences; its headquarters moved to Adyar, India. It strongly influenced modern esotericism and art.
