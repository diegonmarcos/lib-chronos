Christian mysticism is the tradition of contemplative prayer seeking direct experience of God, within the churches themselves: Pseudo-Dionysius, Meister Eckhart, the *Cloud of Unknowing*, Teresa of Avila, John of the Cross and the Hesychasts of Eastern Orthodoxy. Its writings are classics of religious literature.
