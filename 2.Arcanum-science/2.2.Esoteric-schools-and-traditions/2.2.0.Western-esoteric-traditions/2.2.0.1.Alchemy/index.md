Alchemy is the tradition of transforming matter, practised in Greco-Roman Egypt, the Islamic world (Jabir ibn Hayyan), China and Latin Europe. Its aims included making gold, medicines and the elixir of life. Historians now see it as a direct ancestor of chemistry: Robert Boyle and Isaac Newton both worked on it. See also [[eng.chemical]].
