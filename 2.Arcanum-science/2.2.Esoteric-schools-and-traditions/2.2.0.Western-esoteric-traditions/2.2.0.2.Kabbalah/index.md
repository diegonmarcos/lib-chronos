Kabbalah is the Jewish mystical tradition that took shape in twelfth- and thirteenth-century Provence and Spain, with the *Zohar* as its central text, and was reshaped by Isaac Luria in sixteenth-century Safed. Its diagram of ten *sefirot*, the Tree of Life, describes the divine emanations. Christian Kabbalah adapted it from the Renaissance on.
