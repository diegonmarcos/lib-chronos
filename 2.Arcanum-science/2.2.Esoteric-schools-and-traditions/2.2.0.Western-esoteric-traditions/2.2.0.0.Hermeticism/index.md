Hermeticism is the tradition based on texts attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, a Greek-Egyptian sage, written in Roman Egypt in the first centuries CE: the *Corpus Hermeticum* and the *Asclepius*. Their Latin translation by Marsilio Ficino in 1471 made them central to Renaissance thought, until Isaac Casaubon dated them in 1614.
