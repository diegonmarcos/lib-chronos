Witchcraft has two histories. One is the early modern European witch hunts (about 1450-1750), in which tens of thousands were tried and many executed under a learned theory of devil worship. The other is modern Wicca and related movements, founded in England in the 1950s, which present witchcraft as a nature religion. See also [[beliefs.religion-studies]].
