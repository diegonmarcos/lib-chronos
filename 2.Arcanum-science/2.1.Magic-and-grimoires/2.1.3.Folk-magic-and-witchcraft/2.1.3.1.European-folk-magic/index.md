European folk magic includes the cunning folk of Britain, the *benandanti* of Friuli, the *Segensprüche* of German-speaking lands, Scandinavian *svartkonst* books, and seasonal rites such as midsummer fires. Trial records and charm books preserve much of it. Historians such as Carlo Ginzburg and Owen Davies have studied it in depth.
