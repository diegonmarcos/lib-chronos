*Ritual traditions, magical handbooks, folk practice, and the objects made to act in the world.*

Division B of the taxonomy groups the operative arts built on that picture of the cosmos: divination, alchemy, ceremonial magic and its handbooks, folk magic and witchcraft, and the making of talismans and ritual objects. The pages describe these practices as historians, anthropologists and folklorists study them.
