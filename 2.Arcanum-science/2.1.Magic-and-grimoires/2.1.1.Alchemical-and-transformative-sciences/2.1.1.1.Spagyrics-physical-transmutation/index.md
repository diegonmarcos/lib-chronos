tags: arcanum, alchemy, chemistry, medicine

Spagyrics is the Paracelsian art of separating and recombining substances, mostly plants and minerals, to make medicines; physical transmutation is the older alchemical goal of turning base metals into gold. Laboratory techniques of the alchemists, such as distillation and sublimation, passed into early modern chemistry. See also [[eng.chemical]].
