Sympathetic magic is the term James Frazer (*The Golden Bough*, 1890) gave to practices based on two principles: similarity (like affects like) and contagion (things once in contact stay connected). The concept shaped early anthropology and is still used to describe beliefs across cultures. See also [[anthropology.cultural]].
