Spiritual alchemy reads the alchemical Great Work (*Magnum Opus*), with its stages of blackening, whitening and reddening, as an allegory of inner change rather than a recipe for gold. The reading goes back to Christian and Hermetic authors and was made famous in the twentieth century by Carl Jung's studies of alchemical imagery.
