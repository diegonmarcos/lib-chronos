Runes are the letters of the Germanic alphabets (futharks) used from about the second century CE on stone, wood and metal. Some inscriptions are charms, and Tacitus describes lot-casting with marked slips. Rune divination as practised today is largely a twentieth-century revival. Runology studies the inscriptions as language and history.
