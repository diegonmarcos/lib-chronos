The *I Ching* (Book of Changes) is a Chinese classic built on 64 hexagrams of six broken or unbroken lines, chosen by counting yarrow stalks or tossing coins. Its core dates to the Western Zhou, and Confucian commentaries made it a work of philosophy as well as divination. It shaped Chinese cosmology and was read widely in the West from the twentieth century.
