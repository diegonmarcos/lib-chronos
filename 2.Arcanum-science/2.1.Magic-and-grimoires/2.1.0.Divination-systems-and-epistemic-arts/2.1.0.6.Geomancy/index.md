Geomancy (Arabic *'ilm al-raml*, 'the science of the sand') is a divination method that turns random marks in sand or on paper into figures of four lines each, then combines them into a chart. It spread from the Islamic world to medieval Europe and West Africa, where it is related to Ifa and Sikidy. Not to be confused with Chinese feng shui.
