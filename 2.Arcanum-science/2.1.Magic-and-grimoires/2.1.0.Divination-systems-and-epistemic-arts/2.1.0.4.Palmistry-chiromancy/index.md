title: Palmistry & Chiromancy
tags: arcanum, divination, body, history
---
Palmistry, or chiromancy, reads character and fortune from the lines, mounts and shape of the hand. It appears in India, China and ancient Greece, and printed manuals spread it in Renaissance Europe. Like physiognomy, it has no scientific support and is studied as a cultural practice.
