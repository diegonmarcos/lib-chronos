Oneiromancy is divination by dreams, often by deliberate dream incubation: sleeping in a temple to receive a healing or prophetic dream, as at the Asclepieia of Greece. Lucid dreaming, the awareness of dreaming within a dream, is described in Tibetan dream yoga and has been measured in sleep laboratories since the 1980s.
