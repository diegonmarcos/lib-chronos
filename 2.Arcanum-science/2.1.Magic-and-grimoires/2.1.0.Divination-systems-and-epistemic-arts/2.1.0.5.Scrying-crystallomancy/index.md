Scrying is the practice of looking into a reflective or clear surface (a crystal, a black mirror, a bowl of water) to see visions. It is recorded from antiquity, and John Dee's obsidian mirror and crystal sessions in the 1580s are its most famous European case. Historians and psychologists study it as a form of visionary experience.
