Astrological divination covers the branches of astrology that answer questions or pick times: horary (a chart for the moment a question is asked), electional (choosing a favourable moment) and mundane or judicial astrology (forecasting for states and events). Medieval Arabic and Latin authors such as Masha'allah and Guido Bonatti wrote its main manuals.
