An oracle was a sanctuary or person through whom a god was thought to answer questions: the Pythia at Delphi, the oak of Dodona, the oracle of Amun at Siwa. Cities and rulers consulted them on war, colonies and law, so oracles were political institutions as well as religious ones. Modern 'oracle decks' borrow the name.
