Dream interpretation reads dreams as messages, from the Egyptian and Babylonian dream books and Artemidorus' *Oneirocritica* (second century CE) to Freud's *Interpretation of Dreams* (1899) and Jung. Modern sleep science studies dreaming in the brain; the older traditions are studied as cultural history. See also [[medicine.physiology]].
