Sortilege is divination by casting lots, dice, bones or marked objects, one of the oldest and most widespread forms; cartomancy is its later form with playing cards, popular from the eighteenth century. Both treat a chance outcome as meaningful. See also [[math.probability]] for the mathematics of chance.
