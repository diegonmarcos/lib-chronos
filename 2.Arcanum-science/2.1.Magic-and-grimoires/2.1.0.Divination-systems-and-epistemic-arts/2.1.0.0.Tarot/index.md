The tarot is a deck of 78 cards, 22 major arcana and 56 minor, first made for card games in fifteenth-century Italy. From the late eighteenth century French and later English occultists read it as a book of symbols tied to Kabbalah and astrology, and it became the best-known divination deck. The Rider-Waite-Smith deck of 1909 set its modern imagery.
