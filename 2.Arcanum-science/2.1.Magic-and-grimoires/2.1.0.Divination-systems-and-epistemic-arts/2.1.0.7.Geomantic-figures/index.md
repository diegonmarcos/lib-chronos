The sixteen geomantic figures are the building blocks of a geomancy chart: each is four rows of one or two dots, giving 2x2x2x2 = 16 forms, with Latin names such as *Via*, *Populus*, *Fortuna Major* and *Carcer*. Medieval writers tied each to a planet and a sign. The same binary pattern appears in the Ifa system of West Africa.
