Classical omens are the signs read by ancient states: Babylonian omen series such as *Enuma Anu Enlil*, Etruscan and Roman haruspicy (reading the livers of sacrificed animals), and Roman augury (reading the flight of birds). Augurs were public officials, so omen reading was part of government. See also [[anthropology.archaeology]].
