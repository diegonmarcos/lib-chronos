Sigaldry, from an Old Norse word for charm, names the inscribing of protective or powerful signs, and warding the marking of places against harm. Examples include apotropaic marks carved near doors and hearths in old English buildings, Icelandic staves such as the *Aegishjalmur*, and protective hex signs on Pennsylvania barns.
