tags: arcanum, plants, folk, medicine
---
Wortcunning, an old English word for knowledge of plants, covers the lore of herbs used for healing, protection and ritual: mandrake, vervain, rue, mugwort. Medieval herbals mixed practical botany with planetary and magical correspondences. Some of these plants are pharmacologically active and many are toxic. See also [[medicine.pharmacology]].
