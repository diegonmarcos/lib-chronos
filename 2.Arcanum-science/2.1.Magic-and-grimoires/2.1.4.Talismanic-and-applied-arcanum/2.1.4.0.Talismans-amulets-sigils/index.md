Talismans are objects made at a chosen time and inscribed with signs to draw a benefit; amulets are worn to protect; sigils are signs standing for a spirit or a wish. The *Picatrix*, an Arabic manual translated into Latin in the thirteenth century, is the classic text on astrological talismans. Museums hold many examples from every period.
