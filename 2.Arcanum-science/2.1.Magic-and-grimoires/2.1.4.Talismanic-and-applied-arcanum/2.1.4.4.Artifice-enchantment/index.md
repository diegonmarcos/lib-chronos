Artifice and enchantment describe the old idea that made things (swords, rings, automata, statues) can hold a power of their own. It runs through legend and literature: the Hermetic animated statues of the *Asclepius*, the swords of the Arthurian and Norse sagas, the golem of Prague. Historians of technology note how such stories tracked real automata.
