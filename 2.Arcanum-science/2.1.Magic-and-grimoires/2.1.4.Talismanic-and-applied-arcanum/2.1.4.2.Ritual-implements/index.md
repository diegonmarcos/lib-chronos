Ritual implements are the tools of religious and magical rites: wands, knives, cups, censers, bells, mirrors and the inscribed disc or pentacle. In the Western ceremonial tradition the wand, cup, sword and pentacle were linked to the four elements and to the four suits of the tarot. Their forms are studied as material culture.
