tags: arcanum, objects, protection, folk

Protective amulets are worn or hung to ward off misfortune: the Egyptian eye of Horus, the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern *nazar* against the evil eye, the *hamsa* hand, horseshoes and red threads. They are among the most common archaeological finds and remain part of daily life in many places. See also [[anthropology.archaeology]].
