Ceremonial magic is the learned, ritual magic of Western Europe: long, formal rites with divine names, circles, timing by the planets and written instructions. It runs from medieval and Renaissance manuscripts to nineteenth-century orders such as the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Historians study it as part of intellectual and religious history.
