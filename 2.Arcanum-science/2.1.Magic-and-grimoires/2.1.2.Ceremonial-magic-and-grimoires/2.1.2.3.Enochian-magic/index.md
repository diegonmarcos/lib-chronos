Enochian is the angelic language and system recorded by the Elizabethan scholar John Dee and the scryer Edward Kelley in the 1580s, from sessions with a crystal. Dee's diaries hold its alphabet, tables and calls. The material was taken up by the Golden Dawn in the 1890s, and linguists have studied the language's structure.
