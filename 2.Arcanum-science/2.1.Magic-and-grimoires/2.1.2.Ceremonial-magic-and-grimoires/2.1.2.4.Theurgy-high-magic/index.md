tags: arcanum, neoplatonism, ritual, philosophy
---
Theurgy ('divine work') is the ritual side of late Neoplatonism, defended by Iamblichus around 300 CE: rites meant to unite the soul with the gods rather than to command them. It shaped Proclus, Byzantine thought and Renaissance magic, and the term was later used for 'high' as opposed to 'low' magic.
