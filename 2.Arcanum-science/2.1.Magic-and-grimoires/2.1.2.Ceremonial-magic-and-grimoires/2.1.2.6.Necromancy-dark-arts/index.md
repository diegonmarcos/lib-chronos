Necromancy originally meant consulting the dead, as in the *Odyssey*'s descent to Hades or the biblical Witch of Endor; in the Middle Ages the word came to mean demonic magic in general. Curse tablets (*defixiones*) of the Greco-Roman world are a related, well-documented practice. Laws and church courts treated these arts as crimes.
