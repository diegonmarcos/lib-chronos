Goetia, from a Greek word for sorcery, names the conjuring of lesser or demonic spirits, set against theurgy. Its best-known text is the first book of the *Lemegeton* (seventeenth century), a catalogue of seventy-two spirits with their seals. Medieval clergy wrote and condemned such texts, so they are key sources for the history of demonology.
