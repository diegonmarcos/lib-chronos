Solomonic literature is a family of texts attributed to King Solomon, who was credited in Jewish, Christian and Islamic lore with power over spirits: the *Testament of Solomon*, the *Key of Solomon* (*Clavicula Salomonis*) and the *Lesser Key* or *Lemegeton*. The attribution is legendary; the texts date from late antiquity to the seventeenth century.
