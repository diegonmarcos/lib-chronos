Sacred geometry is the belief, found in many cultures, that certain shapes and ratios (the circle, the square, the golden ratio, the Platonic solids) carry a religious or cosmic meaning. It informed the plans of temples and cathedrals and Renaissance theories of proportion. The wiki's Projects > Sacred Geometry explorer shows many of these figures. See also [[math.geometry]].
