Sacred sound covers the religious uses of chant, mantra, hymn, bells and drums across traditions: Vedic recitation, Gregorian chant, Tibetan overtone singing, the Islamic call to prayer. These traditions hold that sound shapes the mind or the cosmos. Musicology and ethnomusicology study them as cultural practice. See also [[music.theory]].
