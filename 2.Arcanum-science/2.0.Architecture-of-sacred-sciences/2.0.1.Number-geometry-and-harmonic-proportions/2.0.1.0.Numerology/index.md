Numerology is the tradition of giving numbers qualitative meanings. Pythagorean schools treated numbers as principles of the world, and Hebrew gematria and Greek isopsephy read words through the number values of their letters. It is a cultural and religious practice, distinct from mathematics; see [[math.number-theory]] for the science of numbers.
