Pythagorean harmony is the ancient discovery that consonant musical intervals follow simple whole-number ratios (2:1 the octave, 3:2 the fifth), and the doctrine built on it of a *musica universalis*, the music of the spheres. Through Plato, Boethius and Kepler it became a lasting image of an ordered cosmos. See also [[music.acoustics]].
