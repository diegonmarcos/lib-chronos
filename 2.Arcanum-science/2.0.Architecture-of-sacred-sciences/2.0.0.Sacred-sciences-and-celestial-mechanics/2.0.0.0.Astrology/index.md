Astrology is the old tradition of relating the positions of the Sun, Moon and planets to events and characters on Earth. It grew from Babylonian omen lists, was systematised in the Hellenistic world (Ptolemy's *Tetrabiblos*) and was taught beside astronomy until the early modern period. Modern science does not support its claims; historians study it as a key part of premodern thought.
