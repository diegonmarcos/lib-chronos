Historical cosmology covers the pictures of the universe that cultures held before modern physics: the Babylonian vault of the heavens, the Greek spheres, Hindu and Buddhist cycles of world ages, and the layered worlds of Mesoamerica. Each joined a physical picture to a moral and religious order. Compare [[astronomy.cosmology]].
