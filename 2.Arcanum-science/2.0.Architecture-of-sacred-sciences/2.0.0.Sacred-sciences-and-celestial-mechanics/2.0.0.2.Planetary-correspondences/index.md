Planetary correspondences are the premodern tables that link each of the seven classical planets to a metal, a colour, a day of the week, plants, stones and spirits: the Sun to gold, the Moon to silver, Saturn to lead. They express the idea of a cosmos joined by sympathies, and they shaped medicine, alchemy and Renaissance natural philosophy.
