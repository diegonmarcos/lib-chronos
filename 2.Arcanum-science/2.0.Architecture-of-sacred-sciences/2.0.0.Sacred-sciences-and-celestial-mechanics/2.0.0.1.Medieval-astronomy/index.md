Medieval astronomy, in Latin Europe and the Islamic world, worked within the geocentric model of nested celestial spheres inherited from Aristotle and Ptolemy. Scholars refined planetary tables, built astrolabes and quadrants, and kept observatories such as Maragha and Samarkand. Their work fed directly into the Copernican revolution.
