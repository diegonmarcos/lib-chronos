*Number, geometry, stars, sound, and the old pictures of the cosmos.*

Division A of the taxonomy groups the theoretical sacred sciences: the traditions that described the structure of the cosmos through stars, number, geometry and sound, and that linked the heavens to the human body and to buildings. Several of them are ancestors of modern astronomy, mathematics and medicine; see [[sacred-sciences.medieval-astronomy]] and [[harmonics.pythagorean-harmony]].
