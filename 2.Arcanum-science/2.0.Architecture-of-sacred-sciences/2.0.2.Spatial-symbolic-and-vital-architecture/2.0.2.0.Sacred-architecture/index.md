Sacred architecture is the design of temples, churches, mosques, stupas, labyrinths and monuments as religious spaces, often aligned with the sky or laid out on symbolic proportions. Examples range from Stonehenge and the Egyptian temples to Gothic cathedrals and Angkor Wat. See also [[art.architecture]].
