Esoteric symbols and emblems are the visual language of these traditions: alchemical signs, planetary glyphs, the pentagram, the ouroboros, the Rosicrucian rose cross, and the emblem books of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. Art history and iconography read them as compressed images of a doctrine.
