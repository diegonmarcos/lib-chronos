Esoteric medicine names premodern healing systems built on ideas of balance and vital force: Galenic humours, Paracelsian medicine, Ayurveda's doshas, Chinese qi and meridians. Some remedies survive in modern pharmacology; the theories behind them are studied by historians of medicine. See also [[medicine.pharmacology]].
